# Bringing a motion back after it fails — renewal

**Short answer:** A motion that was adopted, voted down, postponed indefinitely, or blocked by an objection can't be brought back at the same meeting. The exceptions are reconsidering or rescinding it. At a later meeting, it can be introduced fresh.

## How it works

**Same session: no.** Once a main motion or an amendment is settled, the same motion, or practically the same one, can't come back during that session. The only ways back are [Reconsider](reconsider.md) and [Rescind](rescind.md). A motion that's only "practically the same" counts. Changing a few words doesn't make it new.

**Next session: yes,** with a few exceptions for groups that meet at least quarterly. A motion can't be brought back as new until the *next* regular session is over if, at the last session, it was:

- postponed to the next session
- tabled
- adopted, rejected, or postponed indefinitely while a motion to reconsider was made and never acted on

In those cases it isn't really gone. You reach it the way it was left: when the postponed time arrives, by [taking it from the table](lay-on-the-table.md), or by calling up the reconsideration.

**Some motions can be made again and again** at the same session, as long as there's been progress in discussion or business in between. That includes adjourning, recess, tabling, taking from the table, and calling for the orders of the day. Most subsidiary motions can be repeated once the situation has changed enough to make it a different question.

**Some can't be renewed in the same session** unless they were withdrawn:

- adopting or postponing indefinitely an original main motion
- an amendment
- reconsideration (unless the question was substantially amended the first time)
- objection to consideration
- setting the same time to adjourn to
- suspending the rules for the same purpose at the same meeting

**These don't count as renewing.** It isn't a renewal when a committee reports back on a referred item at the same meeting, or when a tabled item is taken up again at the same meeting.

## Watch out for

- **The same motion at every meeting.** If someone keeps bringing back a motion the group has rejected, the group can adopt a rule against reintroducing a rejected motion for a while, say three months. Robert's says it's generally better just to [object to its consideration](objection-to-consideration.md) when it comes up again.
- **Renewal used to obstruct.** The chair should refuse motions that are plainly meant to stall (see [Stalling and nuisance motions](dilatory-motions.md)).

## At MBC

Each quarterly business meeting (Bylaws Art. IV §2 ¶1) and each monthly Deacon Board meeting (Art. II.B §3 ¶5) is its own session under Robert's default (see [Meeting vs. session](meeting-vs-session.md)). So a motion the church voted down can come back at the next quarterly meeting, and one the Board voted down can come back next month.
