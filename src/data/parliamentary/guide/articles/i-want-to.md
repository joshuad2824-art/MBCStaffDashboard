# "I want to…" — find the right move

**Short answer:** Find the line that sounds like what the group's trying to do, and it'll point you to the motion and the vote it takes. Most of this comes straight from Robert's own table of which motion fits which purpose (§10).

## Bring something up

- **Propose that we do something** → [Make a main motion](main-motion.md) · majority
- **Speak, or get recognized to make a motion** → [Getting the floor](getting-the-floor.md)
- **Do something routine without a formal vote** → [General consent](general-consent.md) · no vote unless someone objects

## Change what's being proposed

- **Fix the wording, add something, or take something out** → [Amend](amend.md) · majority
- **Swap in a whole different version** → [Amend by substitution](amend.md) · majority
- **Hand it to a smaller group to work out** → [Refer to a committee](refer-to-committee.md) · majority

  *Which one?* If the changes are small and the room can make them now, amend. If they'll take a while, there are a lot of them, or the group needs more information first, refer it.
- **Vote on the parts separately** → [Divide the question](divide-the-question.md) · majority, or just a request if the parts are unrelated

## Put it off

- **Deal with it at a set time or the next meeting** → [Postpone to a set time](postpone-to-a-set-time.md) · majority
- **Guarantee it comes up at a set time, even if it interrupts other business** → [Make it a special order](postpone-to-a-set-time.md) · two-thirds
- **Set it aside for now so something urgent can go first** → [Lay on the table](lay-on-the-table.md) · majority

  *Which one?* If you know when you want it back, postpone it. If you just need it out of the way for a few minutes and want to be able to pick it back up whenever the group's ready, table it.

## Stop or limit the discussion

- **End discussion and vote now** → [Call the question](previous-question.md) · two-thirds
- **Cap how long people can talk, or give someone more time** → [Limit or extend discussion](limit-debate.md) · two-thirds

## Keep something from going forward

- **Keep it from even being discussed** → [Object to consideration](objection-to-consideration.md) · two-thirds against, and only before discussion starts
- **Drop it without voting it up or down** → [Postpone indefinitely](postpone-indefinitely.md) · majority
- **Vote it down after it's been discussed** → [Call the question](previous-question.md), then vote no

  *Worth knowing:* Robert's position is that a motion shouldn't be shut down without full discussion unless two-thirds agree. Tabling something to kill it gets around that, and he calls it unfair. See [Lay on the table](lay-on-the-table.md).

## Revisit something already decided

- **Pick back up something we tabled** → [Take from the table](lay-on-the-table.md) · majority
- **Take another vote because people have changed their minds** → [Reconsider](reconsider.md) · majority, moved by someone who voted on the winning side, the same day or the next
- **Cancel or undo something we passed earlier** → [Rescind](rescind.md) · majority with notice; two-thirds without
- **Bring back something that failed** → [Bringing a motion back](bringing-it-back.md)
- **Approve something that was done before the group signed off** → [Ratify](ratify.md) · majority
- **Keep a small, thin meeting from settling something big** → [Reconsider and enter on the minutes](reconsider.md)
