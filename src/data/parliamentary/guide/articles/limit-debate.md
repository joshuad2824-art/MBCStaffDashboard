# Limiting or extending discussion

**Short answer:** The group can change the speaking rules for a particular motion. It can cap the length or number of speeches, set a time to stop and vote, or give someone extra time. Each of these takes a two-thirds vote, with no discussion of the motion itself.

`Second: Yes · Debate: No · Amend: Yes · Vote: Two-thirds · Interrupt a speaker: No`

**What to say:**

- "I move that discussion on this motion be limited to one speech of three minutes per person."
- "I move that discussion close and we vote on the motion at 8:30."
- "I move that Brother Smith's time be extended five minutes."
- "I move that each side have ten minutes, and that we then vote."

## How it works

**It only applies to motions that can be discussed.** It covers the motion immediately in front of the group unless you name others.

**A limit carries over to related motions.** Once a limit is in place, it applies to anything that comes up on that motion while it's in force, like amendments, motions to refer, or a motion to reconsider.

**An extension doesn't carry over.** It only covers the motion you named.

**You can stack them.** Once one is adopted, another can be moved as long as it doesn't conflict, like limiting speech length and also setting a time to vote.

**Some motions are blocked by a closing time.** Once the group has set a time to close discussion and vote, the motion can't be postponed or referred unless the group first reconsiders the order setting that time. It can still be tabled. If it's taken from the table after the closing time, the chair puts it to a vote immediately.

**It lasts only for the session.** If the motion carries over to another meeting, the regular rules apply again.

**It can be reconsidered,** even after it's been partly carried out.

## Watch out for

- **Extending one person's time.** This is usually done by general consent. The chair asks, "Is there any objection to giving the speaker two more minutes?" A formal motion isn't needed unless someone objects.
- **Confusing this with calling the question.** Calling the question ends discussion now. This one shapes how discussion goes.
