# Dropping it without a direct vote — "Postpone Indefinitely"

**Short answer:** Despite the name, this doesn't postpone anything. It rejects the motion for the rest of the meeting without a straight up-or-down vote on it. It takes a majority, and it opens the whole motion up for discussion.

`Second: Yes · Debate: Yes, and the main motion is open too · Amend: No · Vote: Majority · Interrupt a speaker: No`

**What to say:** "I move to postpone the motion indefinitely."

## How it works

**It's a way to reject a motion safely.** Robert's says it's made by opponents of a motion who aren't sure they have the votes.

- **If it passes,** the motion is dead for the rest of the session.
- **If it fails,** the motion's still on the floor, and opponents get another chance to vote it down directly. They've also learned how strong each side is.

**The whole motion is open for discussion.** You can talk about the merits of the original motion, not just about whether to drop it. Members who've already used up their speaking turns on the motion can speak again, because technically it's a different question.

**It ranks lowest.** It yields to every other motion that can act on the main motion, including amending, referring, postponing to a set time, tabling, and calling the question. The only ones that can be applied to it are calling the question and limiting discussion.

**If the motion gets referred to a committee** while this one's pending, this one's simply dropped. It doesn't go to the committee.

**Undoing it.** A vote to postpone indefinitely can be [reconsidered](reconsider.md) if it passed, but not if it failed. If it fails, it can't be made again at that session.

## Watch out for

- **Thinking it means "later."** It doesn't. To come back to something later, use [Postpone to a set time](postpone-to-a-set-time.md). To set it aside for a few minutes, use [Lay on the table](lay-on-the-table.md).
- **Expecting it to end discussion.** It actually adds discussion. To end discussion and vote, the motion is [Call the question](previous-question.md).
