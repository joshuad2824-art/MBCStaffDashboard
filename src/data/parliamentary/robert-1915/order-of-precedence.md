# Order of Precedence of Motions

## Order of Precedence of Motions

The ordinary motions rank as follows, the lowest in rank being at the bottom and the highest at the top of the list. When any one of them is immediately pending the motions above it in the list are in order, and those below are out of order.

1. **Fix the Time to which to Adjourn** (§16). Undebatable. Usually privileged: privileged only when made while another question is pending, and in an assembly that has made no provision for another meeting on the same or the next day. Can be amended. Majority vote.
2. **Adjourn** (§17). Undebatable. Usually privileged: loses its privileged character and is a main motion if in any way qualified, or if its effect, if adopted, is to dissolve the assembly without any provision for its meeting again. Cannot be amended. Majority vote.
3. **Take a Recess** (§18). Undebatable. Usually privileged: privileged only when made while other business is pending. Can be amended. Majority vote.
4. **Raise a Question of Privilege** (§19). Undebatable. Privileged. Cannot be amended. Majority vote.
5. **Call for the Orders of the Day** (§20). Undebatable. Privileged. Cannot be amended. Majority vote.
6. **Lay on the Table** (§28). Undebatable. Cannot be amended. Majority vote.
7. **Previous Question** (§29). Undebatable. Cannot be amended. Two-thirds vote.
8. **Limit or Extend Limits of Debate** (§30). Undebatable. Can be amended — §30 says these motions may be amended, though the list as reformatted leaves that column unmarked. Two-thirds vote.
9. **Postpone to a Certain Time** (§31). Debatable. Can be amended. Majority vote.
10. **Commit or Refer** (§32). Debatable. Can be amended. Majority vote.
11. **Amend** (§33). Debatable. Can be amended. Majority vote.
12. **Postpone Indefinitely** (§34). Debatable. Cannot be amended. Majority vote.
13. **A Main Motion** (§11). Debatable. Can be amended. Majority vote.

> From the dashboard: the 1915 list marks each motion in five columns — debatable, usually privileged, not always privileged, can be amended, and requires a two-thirds vote. Here each mark is written out in words. Motions not marked as requiring a two-thirds vote require only a majority.
